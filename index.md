## Research overview
I am an applied economist specializing in development economics and public policy. My work examines how state capability and institutional design shape local development outcomes in Latin America. I use causal inference methods and administrative data to study how territorial inequalities and weak institutions affect educational and economic outcomes, with an emphasis on producing research that is both rigorous and relevant for policymaking in emerging economies.
